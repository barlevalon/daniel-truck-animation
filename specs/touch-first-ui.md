# Touch‑First UI

## Goal

Ensure all interactions are easy for toddlers on touch devices while remaining usable on desktop.

## Requirements

- Primary controls are large and easily tappable on mobile.
- No tiny text‑only UI; use clear visuals and spacing.
- Avoid hover‑only interactions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- All interactions can be triggered on a phone without precision.
- Desktop interactions still work with mouse/keyboard.
- Layout adapts to small screens without overlapping controls.
